Shared Google Play test group

Join once. Opt in when ready.

The same Google Group can provide access to Dhananjay Tech app tests. You join the group yourself, so your address does not need to be copied into a tester list.

Join the Google Group

Three clear steps.

Use the same Google account in the Group and Play Store. Google controls group membership and test eligibility.

  1. 01

    Join the shared group

    Open the Google Group and choose Join group. Review its membership visibility settings before confirming.

  2. 02

    Open the Play opt-in page

    Open the Highlight Studio testing page with the same Google account, then choose to become a tester.

    Closed testing active

  3. 03

    Install from Google Play

    After opting in, open the Play listing using the same Google account. Availability can take a short time after joining the group.
