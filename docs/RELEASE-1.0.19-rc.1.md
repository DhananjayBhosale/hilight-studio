# HiLight Studio 1.0.19-rc.1

Version code 21. GitHub package: `com.hilight.studio`. The separately built Play edition uses `com.highlight.studio` and targets Open testing.

Changes: root cleanup no longer forks one process per kernel thread, escalates TERM to KILL after about five seconds for the exact same process, and reports `renderer_stuck` and `other_renderer_running` in diagnostics (#19); after a reboot root no longer tries to stop the previous boot's renderer (that call timed out under KernelSU), and false "renderer stuck" reports are fewer; an already-exited renderer or a process ID reused by a kernel thread no longer blocks root retries. Incoming-call signal has a selectable style, default Pulse, and no longer restarts its animation every two seconds while ringing; renderer revision 9 replaces older running renderers (#41). Setup is grouped into five collapsible sections that keep their open state and scroll position across tabs, and TalkBack announces Expanded or Collapsed (#73). Play edition only: support buttons always answer a tap and offer Try again, which re-checks owned purchases; they cannot stay on "connecting" after Play disconnects, and "already owned" grants the badge; tapping while prices load waits, a cancelled pending payment clears "Payment is pending", monthly supporters are remembered offline, and the log names Play's reason for a missing product (#76). No new permissions.

Also changed: Shizuku permission check no longer crashes (Play crash cluster, IllegalStateException in checkSelfPermission, about 20 users on 1.0.18-rc.1); root is re-checked every five seconds while Unavailable; Live status shows Not connected, Paused or resting; Copy LED diagnostics schema 4 adds shizuku and gates blocks.

Also: Restart app button in the Shizuku card; "Color follows battery level" switch for charging (off by default); helper text cut to one sentence or removed across the app (en and ja); disabled "Try an effect" buttons no longer respond to taps; Play only, a redesigned support pop-up (eight animated LEDs, two prices, Maybe later, one line of terms) that shows once on the first open after an update for non-supporters and counts as shown only after it is answered.

Play Console, 2026-11-16 (outside the app): India prices changed to INR 499.00 for `supporter_badge_lifetime` (purchase option `lifetime`) and INR 49.00 for `supporter_monthly` (base plan `monthly`, new subscribers only; existing India subscribers migrated from INR 95.00 to INR 49.00 on 2026-11-16, India only; Argentina, Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan legacy prices left as they are).
