## Changes

This candidate adds optional monthly and lifetime supporter purchases through Google Play Billing
9.1.0. After a user tries five distinct styles, one clear choice offers monthly support, lifetime
support, or `Continue free — no ads`. Every choice keeps all styles available. A purchase adds a
supporter badge; it does not unlock exclusive functional behavior. No advertising is included. The
GitHub flavor remains billing-free.
