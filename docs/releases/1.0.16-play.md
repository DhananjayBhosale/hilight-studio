## Changes

This candidate adds an optional one-time supporter purchase through Google Play Billing 9.1.0.
After a user tries five distinct styles, one clear choice offers either the supporter purchase or
`Continue free — no ads`. Both choices permanently keep every style available. The purchase adds a
supporter badge; it does not unlock exclusive functional behavior. No subscription or advertising
is included. The GitHub flavor remains billing-free.
