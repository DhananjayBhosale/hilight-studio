## Verified gates

- GitHub and Play flavor unit suites, release builds, R8, and Play release lint passed.
- The APK and AAB signatures resolve to the expected certificate above.
- The APK reports the expected package, version, minimum SDK, and target SDK.
- The Play APK contains neither `INTERNET` nor `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`.
- The Play APK contains the ADB helper, Shizuku user service revision 7, and no GitHub updater or
  GitHub release URL.
- Google Play flexible in-app updates are isolated to the Play flavor. Decision regressions cover
  downloaded, available, current, unknown, stopped, and disposed states; both flavors compile
  independently. A physical same-code reinstall confirmed the final app launches, displays the
  Google Play update card, and survives leaving Setup immediately after an update check.
- The upstream physical upgrade from renderer revision 6 to 7 preserved user state, replaced the
  old Shizuku service, reconnected automatically, and completed framework-visible Wave cleanup.

## Known limits

- Google Play's real update consent/download UI requires a Play-installed older build and a higher
  code available to that tester; it cannot be proven by a locally installed upload-key APK alone.
- Incoming-call coverage remains partial and needs representative Phone/VoIP app testing.
- Recording-only camera detection remains unresolved; camera/microphone signals are activity, not a
  verified recording tally.
- KernelSU recovery and the reported affected-device stuck-LED scenario still need reporter/device
  confirmation. Framework cleanup is not proof of physical darkness on that affected hardware.
- Google Play review and tester delivery remain external acceptance gates until Console submission
  is accepted.
