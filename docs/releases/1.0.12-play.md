## Known limits

- Google Play's real update consent/download UI requires a Play-installed older build and a higher
  code available to that tester; it cannot be proven by a locally installed upload-key APK alone.
- Incoming-call coverage remains partial and needs representative Phone/VoIP app testing.
- Recording-only camera detection remains unresolved; camera/microphone signals are activity, not a
  verified recording tally.
- KernelSU recovery and the reported affected-device stuck-LED scenario still need reporter/device
  confirmation. Framework cleanup is not proof of physical darkness on that affected hardware.
- Google Play review and tester delivery remain external acceptance gates until Console submission
  is accepted.
