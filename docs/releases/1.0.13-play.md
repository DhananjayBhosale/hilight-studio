## Changes

The update includes safer root reconnection, an optional Shizuku-disconnect/reboot recovery
notification, and seven additional animations: Meter, Strobe, Heartbeat, Bounce, Radar, Converge
and Glitch. Existing rules, saved settings, output limits and the Play distribution restrictions
are retained. See `docs/FEEDBACK-2026-09-12.md` for the issue and PR dispositions.
