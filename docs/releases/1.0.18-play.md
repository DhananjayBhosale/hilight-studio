## Changes

This update improves renderer recovery around Shizuku manager returns and cancelled permission
flows, retains KernelSU startup and heartbeat recovery, and adds 13 charging animation choices.

The Play-only support dialog now explains why it appears, includes a clear **Skip — continue free**
button, and states that every feature remains free with no ads. It appears at most once per app
version for non-supporters after more than five distinct styles have been used, or when selecting a
sixth distinct style. Existing supporters do not see it. The GitHub flavor remains billing-free.
