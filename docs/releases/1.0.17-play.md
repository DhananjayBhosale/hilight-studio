## Changes

This update adds a permanent Play-only **Support the App** card at the top of Setup and refreshes
the Dhananjay Tech attribution. Monthly and lifetime support remain optional: every feature stays
free and the app has no ads. After five styles have been used, the support dialog can appear once
per app update. Dismissing it or choosing **Continue free** applies the selected style and suppresses
the dialog for the rest of that version. The GitHub flavor remains billing-free.
