Highlight Studio is a controller for the eight-LED HiLight array on the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold running Android 17.

Create solid colours and animated patterns, save presets, and let the array respond to notifications, foreground apps, selected contacts, microphone activity, or camera activity. Quiet hours, Do Not Disturb, Battery Saver, low-battery controls, and enforced runtime limits help prevent unwanted or excessive output.

The app uses access explicitly supplied by you through Shizuku, an ADB-started helper, or an existing root manager. It does not root your device. Privileged access must be restored after a reboot.

The Google Play build has no advertising, analytics, account system, telemetry, or internet permission. Rules, remembered chat names, and settings remain on the device. Notification contents, audio, and video are never recorded or uploaded.

This is experimental software for specific hardware. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Review the setup instructions and safety notes before use.
