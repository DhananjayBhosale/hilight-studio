# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold owners evaluating an experimental hardware utility before joining its Google Play test. They need to confirm compatibility, understand privileged setup, and judge privacy and safety without reading source code.

## Product Purpose

Present Highlight Studio clearly, host the canonical privacy policy required by Google Play, provide practical support, and guide testers from the shared Google Group to the Play testing track.

## Brand Personality

Precise, luminous, grounded. The site should feel like a trustworthy hardware instrument rather than a generic app campaign.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card grids, speculative performance claims, Google-affiliation cues, neon gaming aesthetics, and prominent GitHub promotion. Do not hide the experimental hardware limitations or privileged-access requirements.

## Design Principles

1. Put compatibility and experimental status before persuasion.
2. Use the eight-LED array as the visual identity and information rhythm.
3. Explain privacy and privileged access in plain language.
4. Make every testing step explicit and reversible.
5. Prefer verified screenshots and concrete product behavior over abstract promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support, high text contrast, and text labels wherever color communicates state.
