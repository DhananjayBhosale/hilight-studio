# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold owners who want to experiment with the HiLight
LED array. Some are Android developers or rooted-device users; others follow the guided Shizuku or
ADB setup.

## Product Purpose

HiLight Studio turns the Pixel 11 HiLight array into a configurable local tool for previews, ambient
looks, app and conversation alerts, and privacy-activity signals. Success means the requested effect
is obvious, bounded by safety limits, and releases the hardware cleanly back to Android.

## Brand Personality

Pixel-native, candid, experimental. Copy is direct about privileged access, hardware limits, and what
has or has not been verified.

## Anti-references

- Gaming-RGB dashboards with excessive decoration or technical noise.
- Generic AI-generated card grids and repeated explanatory copy.
- Interfaces that hide safety limits, permission consequences, or renderer state.
- Custom controls where familiar Material controls are clearer.

## Design Principles

1. Make the common path one obvious action; keep alternate transports available but secondary.
2. Explain privileged behavior in plain language at the point where it matters.
3. Use the existing Pixel and Material vocabulary so the hardware experimentation feels trustworthy.
4. Show live state and failure honestly; never imply an unavailable renderer is active.
5. Keep personal notification, conversation, and sensor-activity data local and minimal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Material touch targets and semantics, never rely on colour alone, respect system theme and dynamic
colour, keep motion stateful rather than decorative, and ship English and Japanese copy together.
